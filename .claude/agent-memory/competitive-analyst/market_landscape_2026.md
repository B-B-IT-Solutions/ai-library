Market size: $2.51B in 2026, growing at 29.5% CAGR to $7.01B by 2030. Market is real but not consolidated — no dominant all-in-one platform exists.

## Key Competitors

**PromptBase** — Largest marketplace by listings (220K+ prompts). Pay-per-prompt ($2-$10), no subscription. Creator keeps 80%. Strong SEO. Critical weaknesses: no workflow system, no in-platform testing, no team features. Moderation complaints from creators in 2026.

**FlowGPT** — Largest by users (10M+). Free + Plus $14.99/mo, Ultra $24.99/mo. Multi-model chat built in. Strong community/discovery. Critical weaknesses: almost no creator monetization, no structured templates, no business/professional use case.

**AIPRM** — 2M+ users. ChatGPT browser extension only. Pricing: free to $999/mo. 4,500+ curated professional templates (SEO/marketing focus). Verified prompts. Critical weaknesses: 100% dependent on ChatGPT, no standalone app, no form inputs, no versioning.

**PromptHero** — Image-generation focused (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Sora). Free + $19-29/mo. Millions of image prompts. Not a direct competitor for text workflow use cases.

**Snack Prompt** — Small community marketplace with "Snack It" image-to-prompt feature. No clear paid model. Not a significant threat.

**PromptPerfect (Jina AI)** — Prompt optimizer tool, not a marketplace or library. Different problem space.

## Developer/Enterprise Tools (adjacent market)
Braintrust, Maxim AI, Vellum, Langfuse — these target engineering teams building LLM products, not end-users managing personal/team prompt workflows. Different ICP but signal where enterprise is heading.
