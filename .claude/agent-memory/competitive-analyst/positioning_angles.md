## Angle 1: "Prompts that work like forms, not text boxes"
Core insight: Every competitor treats prompts as raw text to copy-paste. This platform treats prompts as structured, reusable workflows with typed fields. Non-technical users can execute complex prompts without any prompt engineering knowledge.

Headline candidate: "Don't copy. Just fill in. Start."
Target ICP: Marketing teams, content creators, consultants, freelancers — professionals who want to use AI productively without prompt engineering expertise.

## Angle 2: "Your prompts. Versioned. Shared. Reused."
Core insight: PromptBase is a marketplace for others' prompts. AIPRM is a browser extension. This is the only platform treating your own prompts as infrastructure — with versioning, collection organization, and sharing as first-class features.

Headline candidate: "Your team uses the same prompts. Versioned like code."
Target ICP: Small teams, agencies, freelancers who use AI as a work tool and need consistency.

## Angle 3: "More than a marketplace — a platform"
Core insight: PromptBase is a one-time purchase marketplace. This platform is three things simultaneously: subscription access to a curated library + one-time marketplace purchases for premium content + infrastructure for building your own templates. No competitor has all three.

Headline candidate: "Prompts you buy. Prompts you subscribe to. Prompts you build."
Target ICP: Power users and teams who want both a curated starting point AND the ability to create/organize their own workflow prompts.

**Why:** These angles are grounded in actual architectural advantages, not aspirational claims. They directly attack competitor weaknesses with evidence-based differentiation.

**How to apply:** Use Angle 1 for broad consumer acquisition. Use Angle 2 for team/B2B positioning. Use Angle 3 when comparing directly against PromptBase in marketing copy or competitive SEO content.
